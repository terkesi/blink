# Retrieval evaluation, 2026-10-08

Blink did not qualify for team release. On a second sealed hand-written set built to stress large files (jinja and just, every positive needing two to five spans, ten of twelve inside files over 600 lines) Blink completed 2 of 12 against the Reference CLI's 9 of 12 at a fifth of the requests; on the first sealed set (starlette and bat) Blink completed 9 of 12 against 11 of 12, returned source on 1 of 12 no-answer questions against 6, with no error trials, at 14 against 48 requests and 1.6 s against 14.7 s median wall time. On generated questions the two tools are level: 300 against 304 of 360 positives over seeds 11, 12 and 15, at a third of the requests, a quarter of the bytes and a tenth of the wall time, with 5 against 16 false sources on 120 no-answer questions. On a fresh held-out comparison, the retained candidate completed 6 of 11 positive questions and the Reference CLI completed 8. Blink returned source on 6 of 12 negative questions, the Reference CLI on 12. Two Blink trials ended with provider refusals. Blink's median search took 3.06 seconds with 18 requests; the Reference CLI took 18.44 seconds with 44 requests.

The retained change adds up to two follow-up requests that judge helper definitions called by accepted source. It raised calibration from 10/12 to 11/12 in two runs, raised the consumed 45-question set from 15/30 to 18/30, and raised the fresh held-out result from 5/11 to 6/11, with unchanged negative-source and error trials in each comparison. It costs two extra requests at most per search.

## What changed

Main's shared-identifier follow-up plan stays byte for byte. After it finishes, and only while a full attempt timeout remains, up to two more requests judge windows that declare a function, class or type called by accepted source, with the calling window as related source. Keyword and parenthesis patterns find declarations and calls. These requests skip windows that were already planned or accepted, so they can add matches but cannot retract them. They also skip callers that the shared-identifier pass rejected. A deadline during these requests keeps the shared-identifier results.

Default fresh work rises from 16 requests and 512 KiB to 18 requests and 608 KiB. All work, including retries, rises from 18 attempts and 640 KiB to 20 attempts and 736 KiB. The deadline, concurrency, output limits, instructions and thorough mode are unchanged. Evaluation receipts now record every judgment with the donor that accompanied it.

## Diagnosis that led to the change

The receipts for the two remaining calibration misses kept only final probabilities, so the first diagnosis replayed the planner on them. An exact replay later used the recorded initial-pass judgments and corrected that approximation. The Timeout window that `build_request` calls was never planned by the shared-identifier pass. The two cookie windows were planned in its seventh and eighth jobs, with the calling `_merge_cookies` window as donor, and scored 0.45 and 0.01. One miss was an admission gap, the other a judgment failure with the right context.

Two measurements shaped the design. Identical request bodies returned identical probabilities, and the two baseline runs had identical request bytes and judgments on all 24 questions, so live runs are deterministic for a fixed build. Batch composition changes scores. The same window and donor scored 0.71 in one batch and 0.28 in another. Single-candidate requests scored three of four helper windows higher than eight-candidate batches with the same donor.

## Generated question sets

Hand-written sets were too small to see the changes being made (main scored 10/12 on the tuning set and 5/11 on the fresh one), and both are consumed. `scripts/generate-questions` now derives questions mechanically from a pinned repository: the first sentences of a documentation comment with the declared symbol name replaced by "this", gold spans of whole lines at the declaration, a second gold span for a uniquely declared callee in some questions, and cross-repository negatives. A new seed gives a new set. These queries sit lexically close to their gold, so absolute numbers run higher than on hand-written behavioral questions; use them for paired, relative comparison. `scripts/paired-summary` reports per-tool numbers and an exact sign test on paired wins and losses.

Two repositories not used in earlier Blink work were pinned: werkzeug `594452f` and ripgrep `3fce3b5`. Each seed has 60 positives and 20 negatives per repository (160 questions; 9 to 12 multi-hop). The archived driver `compare-v4` is `compare-v3` plus the `generated` split name.

### Evidence pass

Blink judged each window with at most one donor window as context, and its hand-written misses were helpers that only look relevant beside the code that uses them. The Reference CLI reruns selection with all selected excerpts as shared evidence. Blink now ends a default search with up to two more requests that re-judge rejected windows four at a time, with the accepted excerpts (merged, ranked by probability, at most 12 KiB) attached as related source. The pass can add acceptances but never retracts one; it runs only while a full attempt timeout remains, with its own restore point, and only after the card's files are rechecked. Fresh follow-up admission for the earlier phases is unchanged at 18 requests and 608 KiB; the evidence phase may reach 20 requests and 640 KiB, and the retry ceiling is 22 attempts and 768 KiB.

Three builds were measured on seeds 7 to 10 (480 positives, 160 negatives) against main `8ad83a5` (383/480, 6/160 negatives, 0 errors, median 17 requests): the first build `c80afb4` 391/480 (8 wins, 0 losses, p 0.008); after the first review's fix to the card merge `43bf465` 389/480 (7 wins, 1 loss; the loss was one attempt timeout under four parallel runs that did not recur when the seed was rerun alone, 97/120 and no error); after the second review's fixes `4478a62` 389/480 (6 wins, 0 losses, p 0.031), negatives 6/160, errors 0, median 19 requests and 574 KB. Three review rounds; the third passed with no blocking finding. `4478a62` is on main.

### Listwise nomination

Eight independent yes-or-no judgments per request let the model score the right window near zero when its neighbours crowd it out: the same window scored 0.0 in a batch of eight and 1.0 alone, and it was the top choice at 0.85 when one listwise question was added to the very same request. Every multi-window request now also carries one Decisions `choice` question naming the best candidate or `none`. A window whose own probability stays below the threshold but which the model names best with probability 0.6 or more is nominated: nominations lead the evidence pass, and when nothing was accepted the strongest nomination is asked alone once within the evidence allowance. Acceptance still requires the window's own probability, so the listwise answer only decides what gets a second look. Budgets are unchanged; each request grows by about a kilobyte.

Seeds 7 to 10 against the evidence-pass build: 406/480 against 389/480, 18 wins and 1 loss (p 0.00008), negatives 6 against 6, errors 0, median requests 18, median bytes 582,628. The gain was largest on ripgrep (212 against 199). One independent review round, passed. `bc6b5cf` is on main.

### Deepening as the final pass

A probe on the first sealed set's misses (`v3-miss-probe.json`) showed that three of Blink's four missing spans were never judged, sat one or two windows from accepted source in the same file, and score 0.98 to 0.99 when asked alone, so on hand-written questions the gap was finding, not judging. Deepening reads the unread windows of files that hold an accepted window, nearest to the accepted source first, up to four requests and 128 KiB. Placed between the initial pass and the follow-ups it lost 4 of 480 generated positives (the extra acceptances changed the shared-identifier plan); placed last (`66dbb66`) it gained 1 of 480 with no losses, negatives unchanged, median requests 19, and passed review after a recheck gap was fixed. The retry ceiling is now 26 attempts and 896 KiB. It did not change either sealed set's result.

### Larger default budget

The provider never throttled Blink (about 188,000 requests, 32 in flight at once, no 429s), but Blink's own ceilings did: on the fourth sealed set it hit its request cap on nine of the ten questions it missed. `8da285a` doubles the default initial pass to 16 requests and 512 KiB, every derived pass with it (fresh work 40 requests and 1,280 KiB; ceiling 42 attempts and 1,408 KiB), runs 16 requests concurrently instead of 4, and allows 30 seconds; thorough mode allows 64 requests and 2 MiB. A first measurement used a build whose initial candidate pool was accidentally sized from the doubled follow-up policy; review caught it and the numbers below are from the corrected build.

## Next work

On generated questions Blink and the Reference CLI are level (300 against 304 over 360 positives); on hand-written questions about large files the Reference CLI leads 9 to 4 and 11 to 9 after the budget increase, because it still reads more of each file and reads it more systematically. Twelve of Blink's 21 missing spans on the fourth sealed set were in files it had opened but not at the right window, six in files it never opened. The next mechanism is file-level coverage once a file is implicated: a declaration index of the file judged as symbol cards, or a systematic sweep of its windows with the listwise question, replacing lexical window ranking inside large files. The development instrument must change with it: the two consumed sealed sets become the diagnostic set, and generated questions are only a regression check; the remaining losses are werkzeug questions where the Reference CLI's wider output reaches a second span. Refusals are now a window outcome, so the two fresh trials that returned every span would no longer count as errors; that would make the fresh result 8/11 against 8/11, which is a tie, not a win. The remaining fresh misses are behavioral multi-hop questions whose windows score near zero even alone; they need caller or evidence context, not smaller batches. Next: a confirmatory four-seed run of the four-wide first batches at p below 0.05, a budget-neutral file-card request, and a new independently authored held-out split before any release claim.
